Spa & Water

Three things: water, warmth, quiet. Nothing more. Two steps from the cold edge to the warm water.

Water

The pool sits at the edge of the slope so that the view carries on past the rim. The water is warm, the air cold; above the surface a breath of steam. Whoever steps in stops talking.

Image to followThe pool on the slope, steam above the water

Warmth

Stone slabs beneath the skin, warmth that comes from below. A steam bath slows the breathing. Then the cold snow at the edge — through it once, and back into the warm water.

Image to followWarm stone slabs in the steam room

Silence

Up here little makes a noise. The walls are thick, the paths long, the rooms set far apart. What you hear is the water settling, and outside the wind on the ridge.

Image to followA quiet room facing the ridge

Daily from 7 am to 9 pm. From 3 pm to 4 pm the spa closes for an hour.

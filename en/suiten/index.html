Suites

Three rooms, three views. Each shows itself in the first morning light and again by the evening embers. Switch between morning and evening as you like.

Image to followThe Peak View suite, a wide window onto the summit

Peak View

One window, and behind it the whole peak.

In the morning the first light crosses the ridge and moves across the floor. The mist in the valley turns white, the peak standing above it. Coffee on the sill, snow in the air.

In the evening the light goes early. The peak turns violet, then blue, then it is gone. A lamp stays lit in the room; the gneiss walls keep the warmth of the day.

72 m²

Forest Edge

Two larches at the window, the forest below.

In the morning the wind moves in the larches before anything else stirs. Light comes broken through the branches and lies in stripes across the floor.

At nightfall the forest turns black and close. The larches stand like sentries at the window; in the room there is the smell of resin and warm wood.

54 m²

Hearth

Everything is arranged around the open fire.

In the morning the ash is cold. It is pushed aside and fresh wood laid on until the first flicker. The smoke rises up the open chimney.

In the evening the open fire lights the room. The flame travels over the walls, and when the wood crackles everything becomes still.

96 m²
